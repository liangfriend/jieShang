Microphone check lets you confirm that your microphone picks up sound properly. If you're not sure your microphone works before using Singing mode, the tuner, pitch detection, or the piccolo, check it here first.

## How to use

1. Choose the microphone to check under "Input device" (the first one by default). After plugging in a new device, click "Refresh devices" to update the list.
2. Click "Start" and allow microphone access.
3. Speak or make a sound into the microphone. The "Live waveform" and "Level" bar should move with your voice.
4. Click "Stop" to finish. You can't switch devices while a check is running.

## If nothing happens

- The waveform stays flat: check in your system settings whether the microphone is muted or its volume is too low, or try another device.
- You see a permission error: allow Decipher to use the microphone in your system's privacy settings, then open the page again.
- You see "Microphone capture is not supported here": this computer has no usable microphone.

> Other features (such as Singing mode) use the **system default microphone**. If things only work when you pick a different device here, set that device as the default in your system settings.
