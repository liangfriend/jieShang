Play mode is where you listen to a score. It is also the entry point to three other modes: Practice, Beginner, and Singing.

## How to enter

- In "My scores", click a score's card.
- At the bottom of Edit mode, click "Play mode". Any unsaved changes you are editing are carried over.

## Four modes

| Mode | Purpose | Device needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Features](#play-features) (Play mode itself) | Listen to the score, vocal, or accompaniment; play a selected range; transpose temporarily | None |
| [Practice mode](#play-practice) | Play along with the waterfall in rhythm while your accuracy is judged in real time | MIDI keyboard recommended |
| [Beginner mode](#play-beginner) | No tempo limit; the score only moves on when you play the right note | MIDI keyboard recommended |
| [Singing mode](#play-singing) | Sing into the microphone while your pitch is shown and scored in real time | Microphone |

The buttons for the three sub-modes are on the right side of the bottom toolbar in Play mode.

## Requirements for sub-modes

- **Tab and drum kit scores** can only be played. The three sub-mode buttons are grayed out with the message "Guitar, ukulele, bass, and drum scores do not support practice, beginner, or singing mode yet".
- **Every grand staff must have the same number of single staves.** Otherwise you see "All grand staves must have the same number of single staves to merge into one line for practice, beginner, or singing mode". All three modes join the whole score into one long line, and when the numbers of staves differ, the lines cannot be matched up one-to-one.

Sub-modes join the whole score into **one long line** that scrolls horizontally, so every line must have the same structure.

## Leaving

When you leave Play mode or any sub-mode, any score, audio, or metronome that is playing stops automatically.
