In Presentation mode, different elements support different actions. The features for scores, images and videos are all in the **right-click menu**.

## Scores

Right-click a score to open a menu with:

| Menu item | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Play | Plays the score, highlighting the notes being played, at the score's tempo. After pausing, click "Play" again to continue from where you paused |
| Select range | Plays only part of the score; see below |
| Play vocal | Plays the vocal bound to this score, highlighting the score according to the aligned times. Grayed out if there's no vocal |
| Play accompaniment | Plays the bound accompaniment. Grayed out if there's no accompaniment |
| Pause / Stop | |
| Enter singing mode | Enters [Singing mode](#play-singing) with this score and its vocal and accompaniment. Grayed out if there's neither a vocal nor an accompaniment |

- Playback tone: guitar and ukulele scores use nylon-string guitar, bass scores use acoustic bass, drum scores use percussion, and everything else uses piano. If a single staff in the score has a tone set, that setting is used.
- Score playback and vocal / accompaniment playback can't run at the same time; starting one stops the other.
- A score's vocal and accompaniment come from the audio bound to it in "My scores" through [Score-audio sync](#edit-sync), and are carried over automatically when you add the score to a work.

### Select range

1. Right-click and choose "Select range"; a range bar appears on the page.
2. Click a note as the start (green), then click a note as the end (blue).
3. Click "Play range" on the bar to play only that range.

- Clicking a note again resets the start.
- The bar also has "Stop", "Clear range" and "Exit range". The right-click menu also has "Clear range" and "Exit range".
- The range selection ends automatically when you turn the page, switch modes, or start annotating.

## Images

Right-click and choose "Enlarge" to view the image enlarged in a pop-up window.

## Videos

- To play directly on the page, click the play button in the middle of the video, or click the video to toggle play / pause. When you hover over it, a progress bar, time, volume and mute button appear at the bottom.
- Right-click and choose "Play" or "Enlarge" to play it enlarged in a pop-up window.

## Audio

An audio element is a small player: play / pause, a progress bar and time. Hover over the volume icon to adjust the volume or mute. Audio has no right-click menu.

## Text

Text is display-only and has no interactive features.
