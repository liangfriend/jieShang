"No missed notes — survive as long as you can". Extreme mode has no score; what counts is your **survival time**, and the difficulty keeps rising as time goes on.

## Rules

- You start with 3 lives.
- **Any note block that times out and disappears costs 1 life**, including heal blocks.
- A wrong key (playing a note that isn't on the field) costs 1 life immediately.
- Bombs, freezes, and doubles never appear; only **heal blocks** with a green glow do. Slicing one restores 1 life, up to 3.
- There are no combos and no score. The top bar shows the lives remaining and the time survived (seconds.milliseconds).
- **Not affected** by the "Arcade / Endless difficulty" setting on the home page; "Game notation" (staff / number notation) still applies.

## Increasing difficulty

The start is gentle: only treble and bass clefs, only C major, and no accidentals (only white-key notes appear). Note blocks stay for 3 seconds, then take 2 seconds to fade out.

After that, the difficulty rises continuously: the block stay time and the spawn interval both shrink by **half every 60 seconds** — at 60 seconds they are half of the starting values, at 120 seconds a quarter, and so on. In addition, new content is unlocked every 10 seconds:

| Time survived | New content |
| --- | --- |
| 10 seconds | Sharps and flats appear |
| 20 seconds | F and G major |
| 30 seconds | D and B♭ major |
| 40 seconds | A and E♭ major |
| 50 seconds | E and A♭ major |
| 60 seconds | B and D♭ major; double sharps and double flats appear |
| 70 seconds | F♯ and G♭ major |
| 80 seconds | C♯ and C♭ major (by now all 15 key signatures can appear); the alto clef appears |

The range is always D2 to B5 (MIDI 38–83).

## Results

Your result is your survival time. The longest survival is recorded separately for staff and number notation, and you can view it on the [Achievements](#achievements) page.

## Related achievements

Surviving 30, 60, 120, 150, 180, and 200 seconds unlocks "Just luck", "Barely coping", "Pattern mastered", "Sensory evolution", "One with the keys", and "Turn it off" in turn. Extreme mode has no combos, so combo achievements can only be earned in Arcade or Endless mode.
