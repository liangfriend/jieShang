Pitch detection uses the microphone to identify the pitch you sing or play in real time, and shows it on a piano and on vocal ranges. You can use it to practice singing in tune or to find out your vocal range.

## How to use

1. Click "Start" and allow microphone access.
2. Sing or play a **steady** note into the microphone.
3. "Live result" on the left shows the note name, frequency (Hz), and MIDI number; the matching key lights up on the vertical piano on the right.
4. Click "Stop" to finish.

When no pitch is detected, "Listening… try a steady pitch" is shown.

## Silence threshold

Sounds quieter than this value are ignored. The range is 0.005–0.1, and the default is 0.020. Raise it if your room is noisy and you get false detections; lower it if your sound is quiet and isn't being detected.

## Vocal range reference

Six common vocal ranges are marked on the right, so you can compare them with the highest and lowest notes you can sing:

| Range | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Bass | E2–E4 |
| Avg. male | A2–A4 |
| Tenor | C3–C5 |
| Alto | F3–F5 |
| Avg. female | A3–A5 |
| Soprano | C4–C6 |
