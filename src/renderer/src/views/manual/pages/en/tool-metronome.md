The metronome clicks a repeating beat at the tempo and time signature you set.

| Setting | Description | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BPM | 40–240. Drag the vertical scale up or down, or open the BPM panel and use the − / + buttons and the input box for a precise value | 120 |
| Meter | 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 5/4, 6/4, 3/8, 4/8, 5/8, 6/8, 7/8, 9/8, 12/8, C (4/4), ¢ (2/2), 2/2, 3/2, 4/2 | 4/4 |
| Volume | 0–100% | 80% |

Click "Play" to start and "Stop" to stop. The metronome also stops automatically when you leave the page.

- The metronome shares its settings with [Practice mode](#play-practice), so right after practicing a piece, the BPM and time signature here may be those of that piece.
- Settings are not kept after you close the app.
