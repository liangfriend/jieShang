Your collection is the tones and skins you own. Click "Collection" on the home screen to open it.

## Item types

| Type | What it does | Where it applies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tone | The sound of an instrument | "Set tone" in the editor, and the "Tone" button on pages such as Play mode |
| Score skin | The look of the score | Everywhere a score is shown: Edit, Play, Practice, Beginner, and Singing modes, the Teaching whiteboard, the Instrument simulator, note blocks in the game, and more |
| Piano skin | The look of the on-screen piano | The piano on pages such as Practice mode, Beginner mode, and the Teaching whiteboard |
| Performance skin | The look of the waterfall and colored blocks | Practice mode, Beginner mode |

## Browsing and using items

- The page only shows items you **already own**. You can filter by type or search by name.
- Click an item to see its type, level (Level 1 to Level 5), description, and how to unlock it on the right.
- Click "Use" to switch to that skin. The item currently in use shows "In use".
- Tones have no "Use" button; just pick one wherever you need it.

## Hollow performance skin settings

You can customize the effect of the "Hollow performance skin". Its details include "Skin settings":

- **Gradient**: 2–6 colors; you can set a position (0–100) for each color.
- **Flow speed**: 0–100, default 50.
- **Glow strength**: 0–100, default 60.

After you click "Save settings", the changes apply the next time you open a performance page. "Restore defaults" brings back the original settings.
