Ability tests contain four short tests and a practice mode that help you gauge and train your sense of rhythm, sense of pitch, and note-reading skills. Click "Ability tests" on the home screen and choose one.

| Item | What it measures | How you answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Rhythm sense test](#ability-rhythm) | Listen to a beat, then tap it back from memory at the same tempo | Mouse or Space key |
| [Pitch sense test](#ability-pitch) | Hear a note and find which note name it is | Click a piano key |
| [Key note name quiz](#ability-key-note) | Look at the highlighted key and choose its note name | Click an option |
| [Chord recognition quiz](#ability-chord) | Hear a chord and choose its name | Click an option |
| [Practice mode](#ability-practice) | Read a note block and play that note (a customizable note-reading drill) | MIDI keyboard or virtual piano |

## Common rules

These rules apply to the four tests; see [Practice mode](#ability-practice) for its own rules.

- In every test, first choose the difficulty and number of rounds under "Test settings", then click "Start test".
- During a test, the top of the screen shows "current round / total rounds". After each answer, the result is shown for 2 seconds, and then the next round starts automatically.
- When all rounds are done, the overall result appears: "Play again" retakes the test with the same settings, and "Reset settings" takes you back to the settings page.
- The tests don't need a MIDI keyboard or a microphone; sounds use the piano tone.
- Test results are **not saved**: they're cleared when you leave the page, and they don't count toward achievements.
