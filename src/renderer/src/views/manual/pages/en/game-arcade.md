"60-second time limit — chase high scores". Slice as many note blocks as you can, as consecutively as you can, within 60 seconds to get the highest score.

## Rules

- A round lasts 60 seconds. The time remaining is shown in the top-left corner, and the round ends when it reaches zero.
- Normal blocks that time out and disappear **cost no points**, but they break your combo.
- Slicing a bomb costs 10 points; your score never drops below 0.
- Three kinds of special blocks appear: bomb, freeze, and double. See [Main game](#game) for their effects.
- Wrong keys reset your combo and spawn a penalty bomb.

## Scoring

Each normal block you slice is worth 1 point, multiplied by the combo multiplier and the double effect:

**Points per slice = blocks sliced × combo multiplier × double (×2 while active, otherwise ×1)**

### Combos

Normal blocks are queued in the order they appear. Your combo only increases if you slice blocks **strictly in the order they appeared**; skipping a block, letting a block time out, or pressing a wrong key resets your combo to zero. Special blocks don't affect your combo.

| Combo | 0–2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 or more |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Point multiplier | ×1 | ×2 | ×4 | ×8 | ×16 | ×32 | ×64 | ×128 | ×256 |

Once your combo reaches 3, "N combo" appears in the top-right corner. The longer the combo, the faster your score grows, so **slicing in order** matters more than slicing a lot.

> Tip: slice the block that appeared first — the one about to disappear — first, to keep your combo going.

## Difficulty

Choose the difficulty with "Arcade / Endless difficulty" in the home page settings. It is shared with [Endless mode](#game-endless).

| Difficulty | Clefs | Accidentals | Block stay time | Spawn rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Easy | Treble, bass | Sharp, flat, natural; no double sharps or double flats | 3 seconds, then 2 seconds to fade out | Slower |
| Standard | Treble, bass, alto | No double sharps or double flats | 1.5 seconds, then 1 second to fade out | Faster |
| Hard | Treble, bass, alto | Includes double sharps and double flats | 0.7 seconds, then 0.3 seconds to fade out | Fastest |

All three difficulty levels use the range D2 to B5 (MIDI 38–83), and their key signatures cover all 15 major keys. The higher the difficulty, the more often bombs appear.

When number notation is selected, clefs don't apply; all other rules are the same.

## Related achievements

Scoring 10, 50, 300, or 1000 points in a single Arcade round unlocks "Arcade rookie", "Arcade regular", "Arcade ace", and "Arcade emperor" respectively. Combo achievements can also be earned in Arcade mode; see [Achievements](#achievements).
