"My audio", "My images", and "My videos" are your asset libraries. Click the corresponding entry on the home screen to open one; all three work the same way.

## Where assets are used

| Asset | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Audio | Bound to a score as its vocal or accompaniment in [Score-audio sync](#edit-sync); added as an audio element in [works](#work-edit-features) |
| Images | Added as an image element in a work, or set as a page background |
| Videos | Added as a video element in a work |

## Adding

Click "Add audio", "Add image", or "Add video" in the top-right corner and choose files in the dialog that opens; you can select several at once. Dragging files in isn't supported yet.

Supported formats:

| Type | Formats |
| --- | --- |
| Audio | mp3, wav, ogg, flac, m4a, aac, webm, aiff |
| Images | png, jpg, gif, webp, bmp, svg |
| Videos | mp4, webm, mov, mkv, avi, m4v |

An asset's name is taken from its file name (without the extension). Assets can't be renamed after they're added at the moment.

## Finding and previewing

- Use the search box to find assets by name; the list is sorted by most recently modified.
- **Audio**: You can play, stop, and seek directly on the card; only one item plays at a time. Click a card to see its details: duration, sample rate, channels, file size, format, and date added.
- **Images**: Click a card to see its details: dimensions, file size, format, and date added.
- **Videos**: Cards show the first frame and the duration. Click a card to play the video and see its duration, resolution, file size, format, and date added.

## Deleting

Click the delete button on a card and confirm to delete it. Deleting also removes the file, and it **can't be undone**.

> If the audio you delete is bound to a score as its vocal or accompaniment, playback will show "Bound audio not found", and you'll need to bind audio again.

## Backup

Asset files are stored in the app's data folder. Steam Cloud saves only sync the database and **don't include** these asset files, so back up the original files yourself when you switch computers.
