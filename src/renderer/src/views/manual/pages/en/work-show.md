Presentation mode is for showing a work: turning pages, playing scores, audio and video, and circling key points with a pen as temporary marks.

Click a work in the list of works to open it in Presentation mode.

## Layout

- **Left**: the work's name and author, plus a thumbnail of each page. Click a thumbnail to go to that page.
- **Center**: the current page, shown at its actual size; scroll to see any part that doesn't fit.
- **Top**: the toolbar.

In Presentation mode you **can't move or change elements**. To make changes, click "Edit" to switch to [Edit mode](#work-edit).
