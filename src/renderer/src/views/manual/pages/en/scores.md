"My scores" is the core of Decipher: here you can create, edit, and play scores, and then use them to practice piano, sing, and build works.

Click "My scores" on the home page to open the score list. Each score has two main modes:

- **Play mode**: click a score card in the list to enter it. You can listen, transpose, and play a selected range, and enter Practice, Beginner, and Singing modes from here.
- **Edit mode**: entered when you create a new score, or by clicking "Edit mode" in Play mode. Use it to write and revise scores.

## Supported score types

| Type | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Staff (staff notation) | Edit, play, practice, sing; can be converted to and from number notation |
| Number notation | Same as above |
| Tab | Guitar tab, ukulele tab, and 4-, 5-, and 6-string bass tab. Can be edited and played; cannot be converted to another type, and cannot enter Practice, Beginner, or Singing mode |
| Drum score | Drum kit score. Can be edited and played; same restrictions as tab |

## Typical workflow

1. Create a new score in [Managing scores](#score-manage). If you already have MusicXML, MIDI, number-notation image, or other files, you can [import](#edit-import-export) them in Edit mode.
2. Write and revise the score in [Edit mode](#score-edit), then click "Save" when you're done.
3. Go back to the list and click the score to enter [Play mode](#score-play) and listen to it.
4. Practice piano with [Practice mode](#play-practice) or [Beginner mode](#play-beginner), or have your singing assessed with [Singing mode](#play-singing).
5. To combine scores with images, videos, and text into teaching material, add the score to a work in [My works](#works).
