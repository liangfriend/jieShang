Edit mode is where you build a work: managing pages, adding elements, positioning them, and cropping scores and images.

How to enter:

- Click "New work" in the list of works.
- Click "Edit" at the top in Presentation mode.

## Layout

- **Left**: page thumbnails, with "Add page", "Insert", "Delete" and "Copy" buttons below.
- **Center**: the current page; click an element to select it.
- **Right**: the "Tools" panel, for drawing, setting the background, and adding media, text and scores.
- **Top-right corner**: the "Properties" panel, which appears when a score, image, or text is selected.
- **Top**: "Undo", "Redo", "Settings", "Present", "Save", plus import, export and volume.

## Basic workflow

1. Add scores, media or text from "Tools" on the right. Every newly added element is placed at the top-left of the page, at (100, 100).
2. Drag an element to move it, and drag its four corners to resize it.
3. When you need more pages, click "Add page" or "Insert" on the left.
4. Click "Save".

> **Edit mode has no autosave, and you won't be prompted to save when you leave.** Remember to click "Save" often.
