Achievements record the milestones you reach in the [Main game](#game). Each achievement you complete rewards you with a skin. Click "Achievements" on the home screen to view them.

## What's on the page

- **High scores**: kept separately for staff and number notation. Arcade mode and Endless mode record scores for Easy, Standard, and Hard separately; Extreme mode records your longest survival time (seconds).
- **All achievements**: each achievement shows its name, description, and reward. Achievements you haven't completed have a gray icon; once completed, the icon turns colored and shows the completion date.

## When achievements are checked

- Achievements are only checked when a round **ends normally**; quitting partway doesn't count.
- Achievements you earn are listed under "Achievements this round" on the results screen, and their rewards go into your [Collection](#collection) right away.
- Combo achievements can be earned in all three game modes; score achievements can be earned on any difficulty; survival achievements can only be earned in Extreme mode.
- An achievement has only two states, completed or not completed. There is no progress bar.

## Steam achievements

When you launch Decipher through Steam, the achievements you complete are synced to Steam. Without Steam running, the app works as usual and achievements are recorded locally; they are synced the next time you launch through Steam.
