"My works" is where you create multi-page documents for presenting, such as lesson handouts, concert set lists, or practice books with demo recordings.

## What is a work?

A work is made up of several **pages**. On each page, you can freely place these 5 kinds of elements:

| Element | Source | What you can do when presenting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Score | My scores | Play, play a range, play the vocal / accompaniment, enter Singing mode |
| Image | My images | View enlarged |
| Video | My videos | Play directly on the page, or play enlarged |
| Audio | My audio | Play directly on the page |
| Text | Typed directly | Display only |

Besides elements, each page can also have a **background image**, and you can **draw** strokes on the page with a pen.

## Two modes

- **[Presentation mode](#work-show)**: for showing the work to others or presenting in class. You can play scores, audio and video, and add temporary marks with a pen.
- **[Edit mode](#work-edit)**: for adding, placing and adjusting elements.

You can switch between the two modes at any time with the "Edit" and "Present" buttons at the top.

## Files

Works are saved as Decipher work files (.sjw), which include the images, audio, video and other media the work uses. That means you can send an exported .sjw file to someone else and they can import and use it directly.
