"Three lives — see how long you last". There's no time limit; the game goes on until you run out of lives, and what counts is your final score.

## Rules

- You start with 3 lives, shown as 3 hearts in the top-left corner.
- Slicing a **bomb** costs 1 life. The game ends when you run out of lives.
- Normal blocks that time out and disappear **cost no lives**, but they break your combo.
- Three kinds of special blocks appear: bomb, freeze, and double. See [Main game](#game) for their effects.
- Wrong keys reset your combo and spawn a penalty bomb in an empty cell — be careful not to slice it afterward.

## Scoring

Scoring and combo multipliers are exactly the same as in [Arcade mode](#game-arcade):

**Points per slice = blocks sliced × combo multiplier × double (×2 while active, otherwise ×1)**

Combos require you to slice note blocks strictly in the order they appeared; at 10 combo or more, each block is worth ×256. Endless mode has no time limit, so as long as you avoid bombs, you can keep building up a high score.

## Difficulty

Endless mode shares "Arcade / Endless difficulty" in the home page settings with Arcade mode. See [Arcade mode](#game-arcade) for the differences between the three levels.

## Related achievements

Scoring 100, 500, 2000, or 10000 points in a single Endless round unlocks "Tiny endless", "Mid endless", "Big endless", and "Total endless" respectively. See [Achievements](#achievements) for details.
